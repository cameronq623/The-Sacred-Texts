### The Godless Choir (Evil)

**Text Held:** 1st Edition of **[[The Necronomicon|The Necronomicon]]**

**Philosophy:** "If divinity exists, it is a vacancy. A throne to be seized. We will not worship; we will wear the skin of gods."

**Overview:**  
The Godless Choir are the ultimate apostates. Where [[The Chthonic Covenant|The Chthonic Covenant]] are fanatical worshippers seeking to awaken their gods, the Choir are nihilistic usurpers who murdered the lesser Outer God that once possessed their 1st Edition. They flayed its essence and bound its still-screaming hide into the cover of their book. They are based in **The Cathedral of Skin**, a blasphemous structure woven from the timeless remains of deities they have slain and consumed.

**How They Use the Text:**

- **Deicide and Assimilation:** Their sole purpose is the hunting, murder, and consumption of divine or near-divine entities. Using [[The Necronomicon|The Necronomicon]]'s most profound and terrible rites, they don't just kill these beings; they dissect their power, learn their secrets, and absorb their attributes into the Choir's collective form.
    
- **The Un-Blank:** They have perverted the principles of life and soul. They do not use Blanks. Instead, they create "Vessels" from the stitched-together remains of their victims, and then use [[The Necronomicon|The Necronomicon]] to force a screaming fragment of a dead god's consciousness inside, creating utterly loyal, insane, and powerful avatars.
    
- **The Anti-Creation:** They can perform a reverse of the creation act. By reading from their Text, they can "un-write" concepts like hope, love, or courage from a localized area, creating zones of absolute despair and nihilism that empower them and cripple their foes.
    

**Goal:** To kill and consume every last god, spirit, and cosmic principle until nothing remains but them. They seek to become the only sentient force in the void, not to rule the multiverse, but to be the only thing left standing in its cold, silent ashes. They see [[The Chthonic Covenant|the Covenant]] as pathetic grovelers and the [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]] as mere corpse-farmers, while they themselves are the true masters of oblivion.
