# The Lux Chromatica

## Core Functions
**Primary Function: Multiversal & Temporal Stability**  
The Lux Chromatica is a premier multiversal agency tasked with the monumental duty of maintaining the stability of universes and timelines. Their agents operate across the vast expanse of the multiverse, intervening in temporal paradoxes, preventing reality fractures, and neutralizing existential threats that could cause cascading collapses across dimensions. They are the unseen surgeons of reality, mending tears in the fabric of existence before they can unravel.

**Secondary Function: Acquisition & Study of [[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]]**  
The agency's other paramount mission is the pursuit, collection, and cataloging of **[[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]]**—the three legendary books: _[[The Pulse Tract|The Pulse Tract]]_, _[[The Émigré Manuscript|The Émigré Manuscript]]_, and _[[The Necronomicon|The Necronomicon]]_. The Lux Chromatica understands that knowledge is the fundamental force of the multiverse, and these texts represent the ultimate expression of that power. Containing or studying these artifacts is deemed essential for truly understanding—and therefore protecting—all of reality. Agents are frequently dispatched on missions to locate rumored Editions of these texts, securing them within the agency's vast archives to be studied by sanctioned scholars and kept from those who would use them for catastrophic ends.
