
# The Echoes District

> *"We do not build monuments to war. We build sanctuaries for peace. We remember the cost so that others may enjoy the reward."*
> — High Priestess Alisandra, Cleric of Bahamut

## Overview
The **Echoes District** is the serene and contemplative soul of [[Irisia|Irisia]]. While the physical [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendants]] of retired agents are archived within **[[The Iridescent Tower|The Iridescent Tower]]**, this district serves as the public-facing memorial and spiritual center for the Lux Chromatica. It is a place of quiet remembrance, philosophical exploration, and spiritual solace for a population that deals directly with the existential weight of the Agency's mission.

## Atmosphere & Architecture
- **Architecture:** Peaceful and reverent. The district is filled with beautiful, low-level buildings constructed from warm stone, polished wood, and stained glass. Temples, monasteries, and shrines of every architectural style sit side-by-side, surrounded by tranquil meditation gardens, quiet reflecting pools, and groves of whispering trees.
- **Atmosphere:** A profound, respectful silence blankets the area, broken only by the soft chanting of prayers, the ringing of temple bells, or the sound of a gentle breeze. The air is often scented with sandalwood incense, burning sage, or fragrant flowers.
- **Lighting:** Soft, natural light by day. At night, the district is illuminated by thousands of softly glowing lanterns and magical candles, creating a serene and beautiful landscape.

## Key Locations
1.  **The Plaza of Names:** A central, open plaza whose walls are covered in endlessly updating runes and scripts, listing the name and home universe of every agent who has served and retired.
2.  **The Interfaith Sanctum:** A non-denominational building designed for quiet prayer and meditation, open to all regardless of belief.
3.  **The Gardens of Reflection:** A series of interconnected gardens, each dedicated to a different concept (Sacrifice, Hope, Memory, Loss), with paths for contemplative walking.
4.  **The Philosophers' Walk:** A colonnaded path where retired agents, scholars, and theologians gather to discuss the ethical and metaphysical implications of the Agency's work.

## Inhabitants & Culture
- **Primary Inhabitants:** **Partially Retired Agents**, **[[Aasimar|Aasimars]]**, Clerics and Paladins of every faith, **Monks**, philosophers, therapists, and those seeking solace.
- **Culture:** The culture is defined by reverence, introspection, and a shared burden of memory. Those who live and work here are dedicated to helping others process loss, trauma, and the complex morality of protecting the multiverse. Disagreements on theology are settled through respectful dialogue, not conflict.

## Function & Significance
The Echoes District provides a critical outlet for the psychological and spiritual needs of [[Irisia|Irisia]]'s population. It is a place to grieve, to question, to find meaning, and to honor the past without being consumed by it. It is a testament to the Agency's understanding that its greatest resource is its people, and their well-being is paramount.
