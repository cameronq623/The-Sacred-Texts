
# The Sylvan Quorum

> *"The others build with metal and stone. We build with life. And life, in turn, builds them."*
> — Elowen, Elven Druid of the Verdant Circle

## Overview
The **Sylvan Quorum** is the lush, life-sustaining lung of [[Irisia|Irisia]]. A vast series of interconnected, climate-controlled biodomes and open-air preserves, this district is responsible for all agricultural production and environmental management within [[The Spire|The Spire]]. It is a carefully curated piece of wild nature, a place of profound tranquility and vital purpose.

## Atmosphere & Architecture
- **Architecture:** The "architecture" is naturalistic. Graceful, nearly invisible arches of crystal and energy hold up the domes. Buildings are grown from living wood, shaped stone, and woven vines. Streams and pathways meander organically through the landscape.
- **Atmosphere:** The air is incredibly fresh and humid, filled with the rich scent of loam, blooming flowers, and ripening fruit. The light under the domes is soft and dappled, mimicking a perfect spring day. The sound of trickling water, bird song, and rustling leaves replaces the city's hustle.
- **Biomes:** Includes a temperate rainforest, a sun-drenched orchard valley, a vast fungal grotto, aquatic farms, and a managed prairie for grazing.

## Key Locations
1.  **The Heartwood:** The central administrative and spiritual center, a great tree palace where the district's Druidic council—the eponymous Quorum—meets.
2.  **The Canopy Markets:** Open-air markets where the day's harvest of exotic fruits, vegetables, and herbs are sold directly to chefs and citizens from other districts.
3.  **The Verdant Circle Grove:** A sacred space for Druids and Rangers to practice their arts and commune with the carefully introduced nature spirits of the Spire.
4.  **The Hydroponics Spire:** A more technological facility, run in cooperation with **[[The Orange Tower|The Orange Tower]]**, where advanced aquaponics and aeroponics systems grow staple crops with maximum efficiency.

## Inhabitants & Culture
- **Primary Inhabitants:** **[[Elf|Elves]]** (Wood, High), **[[Halfling|Halflings]]**, **Firbolgs**, **Dryads**, **Centaurs**, Druids, Rangers, herbalists, and biologists.
- **Culture:** Life here is slower, more deliberate, and deeply connected to natural cycles. The inhabitants have a guardian-like relationship with their environment. They are deeply knowledgeable and often quiet and contemplative, though they possess a fierce devotion to protecting their charge. Community decisions are made through consensus in the Quorum.

## Function & Significance
The Sylvan Quorum is not a park; it is a factory that uses photosynthesis and magic instead of machinery. It produces 100% of Irisia's food, purifies its air and water, and manages the complex ecosystem within the Spire. It is a critical piece of life-support infrastructure and a vital sanctuary for mental well-being.
