The major operational force of [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]], they are identified by their [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendants]]. Being an agent is a purely voluntary job, they serve as long as they want. Typically agents have lost their original body due to recruitment procedures, instead operating [[Blank|Blank]] from their [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendants]]. All agents have living quarters within [[The Spire|The Spire]]. Newer agents who have little in the way of currency are given a small room in one of the [[The Chromatic Towers|The Chromatic Towers]]. More experienced agents tend to buy nicer homes in the city of [[Irisia|Irisia]].

[[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] recruits the bulk of its agents from the souls of heroes who have met an untimely death. They have also been known to recruit citizens of [[The Spire|The Spire]] or still living heroes they encounter in the field.
