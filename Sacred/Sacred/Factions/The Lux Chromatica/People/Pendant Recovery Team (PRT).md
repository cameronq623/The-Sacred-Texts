
# Pendant Recovery Team (PRT)

> *"The [[Blank|Blank]] is lost. The agent is not. We do not leave our people behind, for our people are not their bodies."*
> — PRT Operational Doctrine

## Overview

**Pendant Recovery Teams (PRTs)** are elite, rapid-deployment units within [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]], tasked with [[The Lux Chromatica|the agency]]'s most sacred and non-negotiable mission: the retrieval of agent [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendants]] from compromised, hostile, or catastrophic field operations. Operating on the core principle that **"The [[Soul Pendants|Pendant]] is the Agent,"** PRTs are authorized to use any and all means necessary to recover these indestructible vessels, prioritizing them above all other assets, including functional [[Blank|Blanks]].

## Composition & Status

- **Elite of the Elite:** PRT members are not dedicated solely to recovery operations; they are some of [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]]'s most skilled and experienced **active field agents** drawn from various [[The Chromatic Towers|Towers]]. They are on a rotating on-call status, ready to drop their current missions at a moment's notice when a recovery alert is issued.
- **Cross-Tower Collaboration:** A typical PRT is a mixed unit, combining specialists needed for the specific recovery scenario (e.g., a [[The Violet Tower|Violet Tower]] infiltrator, a [[The Red Tower|Red Tower]] heavy weapons expert, a [[The Blue Tower|Blue Tower]] strategist, and a [[The White Tower|White Tower]] trauma specialist).
- **Tower Leader Involvement:** In extreme circumstances—such as the loss of an entire high-level team in a critically unstable timeline or a zone saturated with reality-corrupting effects—the leaders of [[The Prismatic Circle|The Prismatic Circle]] themselves may personally lead or compose the recovery team. The direct involvement of a Tower Leader is a rare and grave event, signifying the utmost importance of the recovery.

## Authority & Resources

The mission parameters of a PRT override nearly all other protocols. To ensure success, they are granted temporary, mission-specific access to [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]]'s most powerful and restricted assets:

- **Artifact Armory:** Access to sealed relics from the [[The Black Tower|Black Tower]] vaults, such as reality-anchoring talismans, perception-filtering veils, or short-range temporal rewind devices.
- **Experimental Technology:** Unfield-tested gear from the [[The Orange Tower|Orange Tower]], including prototype Blanks adapted to the target environment, personal teleportation beacons, or weaponry that manipulates local physics.
- **Strategic Intelligence:** Full, unredacted access to all [[The Green Tower|Green Tower]] archives and live [[The Blue Tower|Blue Tower]] predictive modelling for the operational zone.
- **Blank Priority:** Authorization to commandeer any nearby agent's [[Blank|Blank]] for the recovery operation if deemed necessary, reassigning the agent to a new body post-mission.

## Common Recovery Circumstances & Examples

PRTs are deployed in a variety of high-risk scenarios. Common circumstances include:

1.  **The Silent Team:**
    - **Scenario:** An entire field team ceases all communication. Their last known telemetry shows no signs of [[Blank|Blank]] failure, suggesting their pendants are intact but the agents are unable to communicate or are unconscious within them.
    - **PRT Response:** A stealth-oriented team is dispatched to infiltrate the last known location. Their goal is to locate the inert Blanks, secure the pendants from their chest ports, and exfiltrate without engaging whatever threat neutralized the original team. [[The Violet Tower|Violet Tower]] agents are crucial here.

2.  **The Hot Zone:**
    - **Scenario:** A mission goes violently wrong, resulting in the destruction of the agents' Blanks in an active combat zone (e.g., a war-torn [[Universe|Universe]], a demonic incursion point). The pendants are known to be lying amidst ongoing hostilities.
    - **PRT Response:** A combat-heavy team [[The Red Tower|Red Tower]] deploys via direct assault or high-altitude drop. Their mission is to establish a perimeter, suppress all hostiles, retrieve the pendants, and hold the position until a portal can be opened for extraction. Heavy weaponry and powered armor are standard.

3.  **The Reality Scar:**
    - **Scenario:** An agent's [[Blank|Blank]] is lost in an area undergoing severe reality decay or corrupted by eldritch influence. The local laws of physics and magic are unstable or hostile.
    - **PRT Response:** A specialist team equipped with [[The Black Tower|Black Tower]] artifacts and [[The White Tower|White Tower]] purification tools is sent. Their priority is to stabilize the local reality enough to approach and retrieve the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]], often while fighting off psychic phenomena or conceptual entities. Survival, not combat, is the focus.

4.  **The Temporal Snarl:**
    - **Scenario:** An agent becomes trapped in a unstable timestream or a repeating time loop. Their [[Blank|Blank]] may be periodically destroyed and re-formed, with the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]] cycling through states.
    - **PRT Response:** A team with [[The Blue Tower|Blue Tower]] support and temporal-shielding technology is deployed. Their objective is to anchor themselves in the flow of time, locate the precise temporal coordinates of the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]], and extract it from the flow of cause and effect.

5.  **The Captured Prize:**
    - **Scenario:** Intelligence confirms an enemy force has recovered one or more [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendants]] and is attempting to transport or study them.
    - **PRT Response:** An interception team is deployed with a simple objective: ambush the enemy, recover the pendants by any means necessary, and leave no survivors who have learned of the pendants' significance. This is often a brutal, no-holds-barred operation.

## Operational Philosophy

The PRT mandate is simple, brutal, and absolute: **Retrieve the [[Soul Pendants|Pendant]].** The mission is a success if the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]] is recovered, even if every PRT [[Blank|Blank]] is lost in the process. Agents are conditioned to view their own Blanks as the most expendable tool on the mission, to be used without hesitation to shield a recovered [[Soul Pendants|pendant]] or clear an exfiltration path. The knowledge that [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] will move heaven and earth to retrieve them—that they will never be truly left behind—is a foundational pillar of agent morale and commitment.
