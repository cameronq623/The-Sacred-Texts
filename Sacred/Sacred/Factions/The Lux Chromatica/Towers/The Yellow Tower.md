# The Yellow Tower

**Motto:** *"A stable home enables a far-reaching hand."*

**Current Leader:** [[Mayor Greg|Mayor Greg]]

## Philosophy: Foundational Stability
The Yellow Tower posits that the Lux Chromatica cannot effectively police the multiverse if its own house is not in order. They focus on the practical, present-day needs of the organization, ensuring that the engine of its operations runs smoothly. They believe that a well-fed, well-housed, and well-governed population is the bedrock upon which all external stability is built.

## Operational Focus: Internal Affairs & Logistics
The Yellow Tower manages the day-to-day functioning of the Spire and its population.
- **Civil Governance:** Manages the city of [[Irisia|Irisia]], handling everything from law enforcement and education to housing and civic services.
- **Resource Management:** Oversees resource allocation, agriculture, and water and power distribution from [[The Infrastructure|The Infrastructure]].
- **Diplomacy:** Handles internal disputes and relations between the different faction-towers and the civilian population.
- **Logistics:** Coordinates the vast supply chains needed to support agency operations across the multiverse.

## Culture & Structure
Bureaucratic, efficient, and pragmatic. Agents are administrators, engineers, diplomats, and logisticians. They value order, efficiency, and community. While their work may seem mundane compared to other towers, they are the unflinching backbone of the entire agency.
