# The Red Tower

**Motto:** *"A single spark extinguished prevents a conflagration."*

**Current Leader:** [[Scarlett Willow|Scarlett Willow]]

## Philosophy: Proactive Deterrence
The Red Tower operates on the principle that multiversal stability is best maintained through decisive, overwhelming force. They believe in identifying existential threats in their infancy and eliminating them with surgical precision. Hesitation is a greater sin than aggression; a conflict ended quickly and utterly is preferable to a prolonged struggle that risks collateral damage across timelines. Theirs is a philosophy of controlled, calculated violence as the ultimate peacekeeping tool.

## Operational Focus: Direct Action
The Red Tower is the agency's spearhead. Its agents are the first in and the last out of any hostile environment.
- **Armed Intervention:** Specializes in breach-and-clear operations into unstable universes, demonic incursions, and warlord-controlled territories.
- **Primary Military Force:** Maintains [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]]'s standing army and its most powerful arsenals, from enchanted artillery to singularity projectors.
- **Perimeter Defense:** Responsible for the defense of [[The Spire|The Spire]] and its key assets.
- **Pendant Recovery Support:** Often provides the "hammer" for [[Pendant Recovery Team (PRT)|PRT]] operations in active combat zones.

## Culture & Structure
Hierarchical and disciplined. Agents are drilled to act with unwavering decisiveness. They value strength, courage, and resolve. The Tower is organized into specialized legions (Assault, Heavy Support, Rapid Response). While viewed by other towers as blunt instruments, Red Tower agents possess a keen strategic mind for the application of force.
