# The Green Tower

**Motto:** *"To protect a timeline, one must first understand it."*

## Philosophy: Contextual Preservation
The Green Tower operates on the belief that knowledge is the first and greatest weapon. You cannot stabilize a reality without understanding its history, culture, ecology, and the nuanced causes of its instability. They believe every conflict is a data point, and every timeline a book to be studied before it is edited. Action without understanding is recklessness.

## Operational Focus: Information Gathering & Analysis
The Green Tower is the memory and the eyes of [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]].
- **The Great Library:** Maintains the vast archives of [[The Spire|The Spire]], cataloging knowledge from every known universe.
- **Intelligence Analysis:** Processes raw data from field agents, identifying patterns, threats, and the root causes of instability.
- **Cultural & Historical Research:** Deploys scholar-agents to study unstable timelines, providing critical context for missions.
- **Interrogation & Debriefing:** Extracts and verifies information from captured entities and returning agents.

## Culture & Structure
A quiet, scholarly, and meticulous culture. Agents are historians, analysts, linguists, and anthropologists. They value wisdom, patience, and accuracy. The Tower is organized into departments based on fields of study (e.g., Xenohistory, Temporal Mechanics, Cultural Analysis). They are the source of the "why" behind every mission.
