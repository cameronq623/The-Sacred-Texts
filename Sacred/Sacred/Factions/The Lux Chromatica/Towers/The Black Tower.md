# The Black Tower

**Motto:** *"Some truths must be buried so that reality may live."*

**Current Leader:** [[The Keeper|The Keeper]]

## Philosophy: Necessary Sacrifice
The Black Tower operates on the grim premise that some knowledge is too dangerous to seek, and some actions too terrible to take—but that someone must be willing to bear that burden to protect all else. They are [[The Lux Chromatica|the agency]]'s necessary shadow, walling away the things that could unmake existence so that the other towers can work in the light. They believe that damnation is a price worth paying for universal stability.

## Operational Focus: Forbidden Lore & Containment
The Black Tower handles the threats no other tower can.
- **Sacred Texts Acquisition:** Dedicated to the pursuit, containment, and study of Editions of [[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]], particularly the *Necronomicon*.
- **Counter-Eldritch Warfare:** Specializes in combating outer gods, great old ones, and reality-corrupting entities.
- **Containment:** Maintains secure vaults for artifacts, entities, and knowledge too dangerous to exist unguarded.
- **Psychological Conditioning:** Trains its agents to withstand the corrosive effects of forbidden knowledge and cosmic horror.

## Culture & Structure
Secretive, grim, and profoundly isolated. Agents are specialists in the occult, counter-eldritch warfare, and containment. They are psychologically conditioned for their work, often becoming distant and alien to other agents. The identity of their leader, [[The Keeper|The Keeper]], is often unknown, symbolizing the tower's role as [[The Keeper|The Keeper]] of [[The Lux Chromatica|the agency]]'s darkest secrets. They bear the weight of the multiverse's horrors so others don't have to.
