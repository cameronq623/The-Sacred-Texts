# The Violet Tower

**Motto:** *"The strongest wall is one the enemy never knows was built."*

**Current Leader:** [[Violet Willow|Violet Willow]]

## Philosophy: Unseen Influence
The Violet Tower holds that direct conflict is a failure of subtlety. The most elegant and durable solutions are those the target never perceives. Stability is best maintained through misdirection, illusion, and soft manipulation, allowing a timeline to "choose" stability without ever realizing it was guided. They believe a truth hidden is often more powerful than a truth revealed.

## Operational Focus: Covert Operations & Intelligence
The Violet Tower operates from the shadows.
- **Espionage:** Fields spies and saboteurs within hostile organizations and unstable timelines.
- **Psychic Arts:** Specializes in memory alteration, mental influence, and the creation of convincing cover realities.
- **Information Extraction:** Retrieves intelligence without the target's knowledge through psychic means or advanced technology.
- **Assassination:** Executes high-value targets with methods designed to appear as accidents or natural causes, avoiding temporal shockwaves from public deaths.

## Culture & Structure
Secretive, subtle, and independent. Agents are spies, illusionists, psychics, and assassins. They value anonymity, precision, and discretion. Trust is paramount but carefully measured. They often work alone or in small cells, and their full activities are rarely known to other towers.
