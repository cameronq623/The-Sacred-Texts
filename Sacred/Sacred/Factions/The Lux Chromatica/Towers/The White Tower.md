# The White Tower

**Motto:** *"A wound healed is a weakness fortified."*

**Current Leader:** [[Tural Pyreburn|Tural Pyreburn]]

## Philosophy: Purity Through Restoration
The White Tower views corruption, injury, and decay as fractures in the integrity of reality itself. They believe true stability is maintained not just by excising threats, but by actively mending what is broken. A healed timeline, a healed body, and a healed mind are the strongest foundations for a stable multiverse. Theirs is a philosophy of compassionate, yet relentless, repair.

## Operational Focus: Medical & Magical Healing
The White Tower is the agency's heart, dedicated to preservation and recovery.
- **Medical Intervention:** Operates advanced hospitals for treating mundane injuries and magical infirmaries for reality wounds, psychic trauma, and spiritual corruption.
- **Psychological Care:** Provides therapy and rehabilitation for agents dealing with the psychological toll of multiversal travel and combat.
- **Spiritual Purification:** Specializes in cleansing Blanks, locations, and artifacts of residual hauntings, memetic hazards, and eldritch influence.
- **Field Medicine:** deploys battlefield medics with PRTs and field teams.

## Culture & Structure
Compassionate, resilient, and unwavering. Agents are healers, surgeons, therapists, and exorcists. They value life, sanity, and purity. Their work is often emotionally taxing, but they are driven by a profound desire to undo harm and restore balance.
