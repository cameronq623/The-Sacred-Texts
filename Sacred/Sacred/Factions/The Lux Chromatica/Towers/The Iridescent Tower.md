# The Iridescent Tower

**Motto:** *"A single note is a sound. Together, we are a symphony."*

**Current Leader:** [[Opal|Opal]]

## Philosophy: Unity of Purpose
The Iridescent Tower believes the individual factions are a prism; their true power is revealed only when their strengths are combined into a single, focused beam. They seek consensus and synergy, ensuring the agency operates as a cohesive whole rather than a collection of squabbling departments. They are the glue that binds the Chromatica's efforts, preventing internal strife from compromising external missions.

## Operational Focus: Inter-Faction Coordination
The Iridescent Tower is the central nervous system of the Lux Chromatica.
- **Mission Assignment:** Acts as the central hub, receiving intelligence and assigning missions to the optimal mix of agents from different towers.
- **Agent Recruitment:** Oversees the entire recruitment process, from identifying candidates to onboarding new agents and crafting their custom [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendants]].
- **[[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]] Curation:** Maintains the Hall of Echoes, the sacred archive where retired agents' pendants are stored.
- **Diplomacy & Mediation:** Resolves disputes between towers and fosters collaboration.

## Culture & Structure
Diplomatic, empathetic, and organized. Agents are coordinators, recruiters, historians, and diplomats. They value harmony, cooperation, and the big picture. They possess a deep understanding of each tower's strengths and weaknesses and are skilled at finding common ground.
