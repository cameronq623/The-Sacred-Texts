# Soul Pendant

> *"Your soul is here. Your body is there. Your duty is everywhere. But in the end, every soul returns home - or finds a new one."*
> — Final briefing for retiring agents

## Overview
A **Soul Pendant** is a marvel of magi-technological engineering and the primary piece of equipment for any [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] agent. It serves as their official identification, soul repository, multiversal communication device, and **potential recruitment tool**. There are two distinct visual classifications: **Generic Recruitment Pendants** and **Custom Agent Pendants**.

## Physical Description & Variants

### 1. Generic Recruitment Pendant
- **Purpose:** Mass-produced for field recruitment operations and temporary use.
- **Material:** Forged from [[Adamantine|Adamantine]], making it **almost entirely indestructible**.
- **Appearance:** A simple, smooth, featureless **generic disk** approximately 3-4 cm in diameter. Utilitarian and identical in every way to all other recruitment pendants.
- **Chain:** A standard, unadorned chain of woven **Adamantine threads**.
- **Status:** These are **temporary vessels**. Once a recruited agent completes basic training, their soul is transferred to a custom pendant, and the generic one is wiped and recycled for future recruitment use.

### 2. Custom Agent Pendant
- **Purpose:** The permanent soul vessel for a commissioned agent, used for the duration of their service.
- **Material:** Forged from [[Adamantine|Adamantine]], making it **almost entirely indestructible**.
- **Appearance:** **Visually unique and personalized.** Upon commissioning, a new agent describes their desired design to [[The Orange Tower|The Orange Tower]] smiths and enchanters, who then craft a one-of-a-kind pendant. Designs range from intricate metallic cages holding the adamantine core to pendants shaped like specific symbols, animals, or artifacts meaningful to the agent. No two custom pendants are alike.
- **Chain:** Typically a fine, custom-made chain of woven **Adamantine threads**, often matching the pendant's aesthetic (e.g., a silver-like alloy for a silver-themed pendant).
- **Status:** This is the agent's permanent identifier. It is only retired and archived in [[The Iridescent Tower|The Iridescent Tower]] upon the agent's retirement.

**Shared Enchantments & Components (Both Types):**
- **Enchantment:** Protected by powerful **Abjuration** magics, shielding it from scrying, psychic intrusion, and temporal corruption.
- **Components:** Features a small, retractable **injection needle** concealed within its base for DNA sample delivery.

## Core Functions

### 1. Soul Vessel & Sanctuary
- The primary function is to safely house the agent's [[Soul|soul]] for the duration of their service.
- **Inviolable Soul:** A soul **cannot be overwritten**. The pendant's magic protects the integrity of the consciousness within, making forced ejection or replacement impossible.
- When not interfaced with a [[Blank|Blank]], the soul exists within a simulated, comforting environment within the pendant.
- **Critical Protocol:** If an agent's active [[Blank|Blank]] suffers catastrophic failure or death, the agent's consciousness immediately and automatically **retreats into the Soul Pendant**, preventing true death. The agent remains conscious within the pendant until it is placed on a new [[Blank|Blank]].
- The soul is protected from all external threats, making agents highly resistant to possession, soul-based attacks, and memetic hazards.

### 2. Body Interface & DNA Encoding
- The pendant is designed to directly interface with a [[Blank|Blank]].
- Upon contact with a [[Blank|Blank]]'s integration port, the pendant:
    1.  **Injects the DNA Sample:** The retractable adamantine needle extends, introducing the stored DNA sample into the [[Blank|Blank]]'s system. This reforms the [[Blank|Blank]]'s biology to match the agent's original form (or a spliced variant on file).
    2.  **Syncs the Soul:** Uploads the agent's consciousness, providing the [[Blank|Blank]]'s higher brain functions.
- Removing the pendant from an active [[Blank|Blank]] immediately severs the connection, rendering the body comatose.

### 3. Multiversal Communication & Shared Access
- Functions as a cross-dimensional comms device.
- **Capabilities:**
    - Wireless voice transmission.
    - Augmented reality tagging and friendly identification.
    - Real-time intelligence and mission updates from command in [[The Spire|The Spire]].
- Communication can cross universal bounds, allowing constant contact regardless of the agent's location in the multiverse.
- **Temporary Access Granting:** An agent conscious within a pendant can **temporarily allow a non-agent wearing it** to use the basic communication functions (voice transmission/reception). In this state:
    - The non-agent can hear and speak through the pendant.
    - The agent inside can choose to communicate directly with the wearer, creating a two-way dialogue.
    - This is a standard protocol for coordinating with local allies without revealing an agent's presence in a [[Blank|Blank]].

### 4. Posthumous Recruitment
- **Field Recruitment:** Agents may bestow a **generic, empty Soul Pendant** upon a suitable non-agent encountered during missions. These are typically individuals of exceptional skill, character, or knowledge who cannot be recruited conventionally (e.g., due to local commitments, destined death in their timeline, or [[Universe|Universe]]-specific obligations).
- **The Pact:** The recipient agrees to wear the pendant. Upon their **natural or destined death** in their home [[Universe|Universe]], the pendant automatically:
    1.  **Captures their Soul**
    2.  **Records their DNA**
- **The Offer:** The individual's consciousness awakens within the pendant's simulation. A representative from [[The Iridescent Tower|The Iridescent Tower]] (often via the communication function) explains their situation and formally offers them recruitment into [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]].
- **Commissioning:** If the offer is accepted, the recruit is brought to [[The Spire|The Spire]]. Their soul is transferred from the generic pendant into their new, **custom-designed pendant**, and they begin their service as a full agent.
- **Refusal:** If the offer is refused, the soul is immediately processed under the standard **Retirement Protocol**, returning it to their [[Universe|Universe]] of origin (or the closest variant) for its destined afterlife.

## Recovery Protocols
- **Priority One Asset:** [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] operates on the fundamental principle that **agent recovery is paramount**. [[Blank|Blank]] are expendable resources; the Soul Pendant and the consciousness it contains are irreplaceable.
- **Pendant Recovery Teams (PRTs):** Specialized rapid-response units are on constant standby. In the event of a field team experiencing heavy casualties or operational failure, a PRT is immediately dispatched.
- **PRT Mission Parameters:** 
    - Locate and secure all Soul Pendants from the operational zone.
    - Extract pendants by any means necessary; [[Blank|Blank]] are to be considered lost assets and are not to be recovered if doing so jeopardizes pendant retrieval.
    - Return pendants to [[The Spire|The Spire]] for agent re-bodying and debriefing.

## Usage & Protocols
- **For Agents:** The custom pendant, worn on its adamantine chain, is their literal lifeline and personal identifier. Its near-indestructibility ensures an agent's soul can almost always be recovered.
- **For Non-Agents (Generic Pendant):** Used for posthumous recruitment and basic communication functions.
- **For Non-Agents (Occupied Pendant):** Placing an occupied pendant on a living person is a **valid operational strategy**. The agent inside can use the wearer as a local proxy, granting them communication access. The inviolable nature of the soul ensures no risk to the agent.

## Retirement Protocol & Final Journey
The decommissioning of an agent and their custom pendant is a sacred rite performed by [[The Iridescent Tower|The Iridescent Tower]].

1.  **Soul Return:** Upon an agent's retirement, the soul within the custom pendant is **released and returned to its [[Universe|Universe]] of origin**. There, it is destined to ascend to whatever afterlife it was originally meant for, fulfilling the natural cycle.
2.  **Contingency for Lost Origins:** If the agent's home [[Universe|Universe]] no longer exists, the Tower's harmonists use complex astral cartography to locate the **closest possible variant [[Universe|Universe]]** to serve as a proxy for the soul's final journey.
3.  **Pendant Archiving:** The now-empty, physically indestructible **Custom Soul Pendant** is permanently stored in the **Hall of Echoes** within [[The Iridescent Tower|The Iridescent Tower]]. It serves as a memorial to the agent's service, a testament etched in adamantine. Generic pendants are simply wiped and returned to inventory.
