
# Blank

> *"A key without a lock. A vessel without wine. Awaiting its purpose. Expendable, but essential."*
> — Orange Tower Aphorism

## Overview
A **Blank** is a biologically engineered humanoid body, mass-produced by [[The Orange Tower|The Orange Tower]] to serve as a physical vessel for [[The Lux Chromatica|Lux Chromatica]] agents. They are the crucial physical component that allows agents to operate across disparate universes with incompatible physical laws. They are considered **expendable equipment**; the agent's true self is preserved within their near-indestructible [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]].

## Biological Specifications

### Physiology
- **Classification:** Humanoid, bipedal.
- **Biology:** Fully organic, requiring sustenance, oxygen, and sleep to maintain function, much like a standard human body.
- **Key Feature - Incomplete DNA:** The genome is intentionally unfinished, lacking coded instructions for specific phenotypic expression (appearance, voice, etc.). It awaits the template provided by a [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]].
- **Key Feature - No Cerebral Cortex:** The brainstem manages autonomic functions (breathing, heartbeat), but the body lacks higher brain functions, consciousness, or a soul. It is biologically alive but existentially empty.


## Function & Syncing Process
The Blank is inert until activated by a [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]].

1.  **Application:** An agent (or a handler) places the [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]] onto the Blank's interface port.
2.  **DNA Injection & Encoding:** The [[Soul Pendants|pendant]]'s adamantine needle extends, injecting the stored DNA sample directly into the Blank's core system. The Blank's biology rapidly reforms itself, growing to match the agent's original appearance, vocal cords, fingerprints, etc., in a matter of seconds.
3.  **Soul Syncing:** The agent's soul is transferred from the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]] and synced with the new body. The soul provides the consciousness, memories, and personality—effectively acting as the body's cerebral cortex.
4.  **Activation:** The Blank opens its eyes, now a perfect physical copy of the agent, ready for operation.

## Survival Protocol & Recovery Priority
- **Body Death is Not Final:** The near-indestructibility of the [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]] is the agent's ultimate safeguard. If a Blank is killed, the agent's consciousness retreats into the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]]. The Blank is a loss, but the agent survives and can be transferred to a new body.
- **Expendable Asset Doctrine:** Blanks are explicitly classified as **expendable field equipment**. Their loss is acceptable if it ensures the survival of the agent's consciousness and the recovery of their [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]].
- **[[Soul Pendants|Pendant]] Recovery Over Blank Recovery:** In casualty scenarios, [[Pendant Recovery Team (PRT)|Pendant Recovery Teams]] are deployed with orders to retrieve [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]] above all else. Damaged or inoperable Blanks are to be left behind or destroyed to prevent enemy capture and analysis. A Blank is easily replaced; an agent is not.
- **Comatose State:** Removing the [[Soul Pendants|Soul Pendant]] from an active Blank immediately severs the soul-body connection. The body will drop, comatose but biologically alive, until the [[Soul Pendants|pendant]] is reapplied or the body expires from neglect.

## Types & Modifications
The Orange Tower produces specialized Blanks for extreme environments:
- **Aquatic Series:** Gills, webbed digits, enhanced pressure tolerance.
- **Void Series:** Radiation shielding, nutrient recycling, ability to withstand vacuum.
- **High-Gravity Series:** Denser musculature and skeletal structure.
- **Spliced Blanks:** Custom-built using spliced DNA to provide non-human traits (e.g., feline eyes for low-light vision, altered pigmentation for camouflage).

## Limitations & Protocols
- **Not Clones:** Blanks are generic templates, not clones. They only take on an agent's appearance upon sync.
- **Disposal:** Decommissioned, damaged, or unrecovered Blanks are considered bio-hazardous material. If they cannot be retrieved for recycling in the [[The Infrastructure|Infrastructure]], they are to be disintegrated in the field to deny enemies any sample of [[The Lux Chromatica|Lux Chromatica]] biotechnology.
