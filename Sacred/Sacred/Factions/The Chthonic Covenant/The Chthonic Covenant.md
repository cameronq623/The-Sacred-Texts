# The Chthonic Covenant

> "From the Silence, We Speak. From the Void, We Command."

A secretive, fanatical cult that operates as a parasitic theocracy. Devotees of the slumbering **Outer Gods** and **Great Old Ones**—eldritch beings existing outside conventional reality. Their ultimate goal is not profit or stability, but the orchestration of a universal **"Unraveling"**—a glorious apocalypse to shatter dimensional boundaries and awaken their sleeping masters.

## Overview

The Covenant views [[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]] not as artifacts to be studied, but as divine instruments of the Unraveling. Their holy grail is the Prime [[The Necronomicon|Necronomicon]], believed to be the literal voice of their gods, containing the rites and true names needed to break reality itself.

## Operations & Methodology

Their work is a form of anti-creation, a sacred duty to sow chaos and degrade the multiverse.

### Desecration (The "Unweavers")
- **Reality Bleeds:** Using profane geometries and rituals from lower Editions of [[The Necronomicon|Necronomicon]], they create localized "wounds" in reality where physics break down.
- **Cult Seeding:** They manipulate susceptible individuals within a target [[Universe|Universe]], gifting them corrupted fragments of knowledge (often 4th or 5th Editions of [[The Necronomicon|Necronomicon]]) to found doomsday cults.

### The Grand Apostasy (The "Choir of the Final Word")
- **[[The Necronomicon|Necronomicon]]'s Call:** Their existence is geared towards locating the Prime [[The Necronomicon|Necronomicon]]. Reading from it is believed to act as a clarion call to rouse the Great Old Ones.
- **Corrupting the Pure:** They seek other Texts to defile them. Injecting the blasphemous knowledge of the [[The Necronomicon|Necronomicon]] into [[The Pulse Tract|The Pulse Tract]] could rewrite reality with madness. Using [[The Émigré Manuscript|The Émigré Manuscript]] could create an undead army or pervert life and death.

## Culture & Structure

A hierarchy of madness, where power is measured by proximity to the void and sacrificed sanity.

- **[[The Sleeper|The Sleeper]]:** The faction's leader—an entity of unclear origin (rumored to be an ascended mortal, but more likely an avatar of a lesser Outer God). Resides in a non-Euclidean citadel in a dying [[Universe|Universe]].
- **The Echoes (High Priests):** A circle of high priests consumed by forbidden knowledge. Their forms are shifting and unstable. They interpret the Sleeper's will.
- **The Marked (Field Agents):** Operatives "blessed" with a psychic **Mark** that erodes the soul but grants resistance to reality-warping effects and the ability to perceive cosmic geometry.
- **The Flock:** Countless cultists across innumerable worlds—pawns unaware of the larger multiversal game, but essential for creating belief and despair.

## Notable Assets & Technology

- **The Mark:** A psychic implant for communication through the "Silence," provides limited protection from physics, and transforms the user for oblivion.
- **Onierophage Engines:** Vessels that "swim" through the multiverse by consuming the dream-stuff of sleeping beings, leaving behind planets plagued by eternal nightmares.
- **Weapons of Madness:** Arms that damage the mind and body—projectors that induce despair, blades that cut timeline connections, sonic devices that recite forbidden true names.

## Relationships

### [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]]
- **Status:** Active, Ideological War.
- **Nature:** Order & Stability vs. Chaos & Oblivion.
- **Interaction:** Direct and violent conflict. Lux agents terminate Covenant operatives on sight and work to seal their "Reality Bleeds."

### [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]]
- **Status:** Ambivalent, Transactional Hostility.
- **Nature:** Dictated by which [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]] division is involved.
	- **[[Reclamation Division|Reclamation Division]]:** Actively hostile. Views them as vandals who ruin salvage.
	- **[[Strategic Acquisition Division|Strategic Acquisition Division]]:** Opportunistically positive. Views their chaos as a profitable market opportunity.
- **Interaction:** Ranges from open conflict to shady back-alley deals.
