# Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)

> **Motto:** *"Saving the Past. Supplying the Future."*
> **Prime Directive:** *"The Balance of Accounts, not the Balance of Power."*

## Overview

The **Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)** is the multiverse's premier salvager, appraiser, and auctioneer of cosmic remains. This vast, multi-temporal entity operates on a foundational, amoral principle: **entropy is the ultimate profit engine**. The EEA exists to manage the asset liquidation of dying realities with brutal efficiency.

Internally, the corporation is a vessel for a silent, ideological cold war between its two primary operational divisions, whose opposing philosophies are shaped by their relationships with the other major powers. The corporation is a delicate ecosystem of conflict, united only by a shared ledger.

## Internal Divisions & The Silent War

The EEA is fractured by a fundamental schism, fought not with weapons but with bureaucratic maneuvering, resource allocation, and strategic misinformation.

### [[Reclamation Division|Reclamation Division]] (The "Vultures")
- **Philosophy:** "Harvest the corpse, don't murder the patient." Believes in a sustainable model of resource acquisition from naturally deceased universes.
- **Antagonism:** Views [[The Chthonic Covenant|The Chthonic Covenant]] as wasteful competitors who ruin prime salvage with their chaotic rituals. They wage a silent war of **containment**.
- **Methods:** Economic sabotage, "passive" interference with Covenant rituals, and hoarding intelligence on metaphysical contamination.

### [[Strategic Acquisition Division|Strategic Acquisition Division]] (The "Reapers")
- **Philosophy:** "Create the market, then supply it." Believes the greatest profit lies in the accelerated harvesting of high-value assets from *living* realities.
- **Antagonism:** Views [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] as sentimental obstructionists. They engage in **direct provocation**.
- **Methods:** Bureaucratic obfuscation, targeting Lux-protected assets, and information warfare.

### [[The Board of Directors|The Board of Directors]]
- A shadowy council that operates from a customized pocket dimension, **[[The Ledger|The Ledger]]**.
- Views the internal conflict as a necessary checks-and-balances system.
- Adjudicates disputes and sets corporate policy based solely on profit potential.

### [[Compliance Division|Compliance Division]]
- The internal security force.
- Tasked with enforcing corporate policy, terminating internal threats, and dealing with external "nuisances" (e.g., Covenant agents who damage corporate property).

## Operations & Methodology

- **Reclamation Operations:** Post-mortem asset recovery using automated **[[Reclamation Division|Harvester Drones]]** and mobile **[[Forge-Stations|Forge-Stations]]**.
- **Strategic Acquisition Operations:** "Pre-death" asset stripping by **[[Strategic Acquisition Division|Reaper Teams]]** to acquire **[[Keystone Assets|Keystone Assets]]**.
- **Official Stance:** They claim to not cause death, but merely "accelerate the inheritance process" of terminally ill realities.

## The Pursuit of [[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]]

A primary, clandestine objective across all divisions is the acquisition of **[[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]]**—the legendary tomes known as **[[The Pulse Tract|The Pulse Tract]]**, **[[The Émigré Manuscript|The Émigré Manuscript]]**, and **[[The Necronomicon|The Necronomicon]]**. Each division pursues them for different reasons, viewing them as the ultimate high-value assets.

- **Strategic Value:** The EEA classifies the Texts not as books, but as **[[Reality Assets|Reality Assets]]**—unique instruments capable of altering market fundamentals on a multiversal scale. Securing an Edition, especially a high-numbered one, is a top-priority corporate objective.
- **Reclamation's Goal:** The Vultures seek to secure Texts from dead or dying universes, viewing them as historical artifacts of immense value to be preserved, studied, and eventually sold to the highest bidder (often scholarly or neutral parties). They see the Texts as the ultimate source of **[[Stable Knowledge|Stable Knowledge]]**.
- **Acquisition's Goal:** The Reapers actively hunt for Texts within stable realities. They aim to acquire them to manipulate markets, create demand for new services (e.g., "reality stabilization" post-extraction), or wield their power directly in corporate warfare. They see the Texts as the ultimate **[[Catalytic Asset|Catalytic Asset]]**.
- **The Board's Directive:** The Board sees the complete set of Prime Texts as the ultimate leverage over both [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] (who seeks to contain them) and [[The Chthonic Covenant|The Chthonic Covenant]] (who seeks to use them). Ownership would grant the EEA unprecedented influence over the multiversal balance of power, and thus, its economy.

## Goods & Services Catalog

The EEA's product lines are vast. Each division favors goods that validate its philosophy.

### Energy & Raw Materials
- **[[Singularity Cores|Singularity Cores]]**
    - *Vultures:* "Entropy-Pure" cores. Stable. Sold to stable civilizations and the Lux.
    - *Reapers:* "Catalyst" cores. Volatile, high-yield. Sold for weapons and engines.
- **[[Anima|Anima]]** (The essence of "life")
    - *Vultures:* "Palliative Extractions." For healing and creation.
    - *Reapers:* "Primordial Anima." Volatile and terror-imbued. Prized by [[The Chthonic Covenant|the Covenant]].
- **[[Reality Lattices|Reality Lattices]]** (Crystalline matrices of physical laws)
    - *Vultures:* "Stable Lattices." For patching reality or study.
    - *Reapers:* "Volatile Lattices." Weaponized to create zones of altered physics.

### Artifacts & Biological Matter
- **[[Artifacts of Lost Worlds|Artifacts of Lost Worlds]]**
    - *Vultures:* Pristine cultural archives.
    - *Reapers:* "Blood-soaked" relics with dark provenance.
- **Conceptual Embodiments** (e.g., "Hope," "Mercy")
    - *Vultures:* Traded as historical curiosities.
    - *Reapers:* Actively extracted from living worlds to create markets for despair.
- **Genetic Archives**
    - A key conflict point. Reapers splice and edit them for custom clients; Vultures see this as desecration.

### Services
- **Premium [[Blank|Blank]] Production**
    - Offered *exclusively* to [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] under a strict, secret contract. A major source of tension.
- **Reality Disposal**
    - The safe "disposal" of dangerously unstable universes. Clients have included both the Lux and [[The Chthonic Covenant|the Covenant]].
- **Weapons of Mass Destruction**
    - *Reaper Domain:* Trafficks in planet-killers and reality bombs from dead universes.
    - *Vulture Stance:* Argues for dismantling them into raw materials.

### The "Forbidden Auction"
- **[[Keystone Assets|Keystone Assets]]** are never listed publicly. Auctioned in secret to a select list of ultra-wealthy, powerful, and often insane clients. The ultimate symbol of the EEA's amorality.
- **Text Editions:** Better editions of **[[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]]** are among the most common and sought-after items in the Forbidden Auction. The pursuit of a rumored **1st Edition** can consume an entire division's quarterly budget.

## Corporate Culture

- **Recruitment:** Employees are recruited from across the multiverse with promises of immense wealth for a lifetime contract.
- **The Suits:** Management is divided into Vulture or Reaper-aligned factions.
- **The Grunts:** Field agents develop professional detachment. They suffer from high rates of **[[Existential Psychosis|Existential Psychosis]]** and **[[Reality Sickness|Reality Sickness]]**.
- **Relationships:** 
    - **[[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]]:** A necessary, if distasteful, client and a meddlesome regulatory body.
    - **[[The Chthonic Covenant|The Chthonic Covenant]]:** Hazardous clients and wasteful competitors.

## Summary

The EEA is the ultimate amoral center in the multiverse:
- They **supply** the **[[The Lux Chromatica|Lux Chromatica]]** with tools for preservation.
- They **enable** the **[[The Chthonic Covenant|Chthonic Covenant]]** by selling them tools for destruction.
- They **profit** from both, all while their own divisions wage a silent war over the corporation's soul.
- They relentlessly pursue **[[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]]**, seeing them not as objects of wisdom or power, but as the ultimate commodities to be acquired, controlled, and sold to reshape the multiversal market itself.
