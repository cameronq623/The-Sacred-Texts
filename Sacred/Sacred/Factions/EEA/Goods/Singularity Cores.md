# Singularity Cores

> **Classification:** Reality-Anchored Entropic Energy Source
> **[[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]] Designation:** Asset Class-Ω (Omega)

## Overview

A **Singularity Core** is an infinitely dense power source harvested from the collapsed stellar remnants or reality anchors of terminated universes. They are the most stable and potent energy source known to exist within the multiverse, serving as the cornerstone of [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA's]] energy and raw materials market. Functioning as a contained "piece" of a dead [[Universe|Universe]]'s final state, they represent the ultimate expression of entropic conversion into usable power.

## Physical Description

A dormant Core appears as a perfectly smooth, featureless sphere of obsidian, approximately 30 centimeters in diameter. It is cool to the touch and possesses a seemingly infinite mass, requiring specialized [[Gravitic Suspension Fields|Gravitic Suspension Fields]] for transport and handling. When active, the core's surface becomes non-reflective, a void blacker than black, from which no light or energy can escape without specialized siphon technology. A faint, sub-audible hum—a literal **[[Entropic Pulse|Entropic Pulse]]**—can be detected in its immediate vicinity, a signature that [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] uses to track illegal Core activity.

## Creation & Harvesting

The extraction and creation of a stable Core is a complex process handled exclusively by the [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]]'s **[[Reclamation Division|Reclamation Division]]**.

1.  **Identification:** [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]] Chronological Surveyors identify a [[Universe|Universe]] in its final state of heat death or post-cataclysmic collapse.
2.  **Targeting:** Harvester Drones are deployed to locate and isolate the [[Universe|Universe]]'s central, anchoring singularity—often the collapsed remnant of its final supermassive black hole or the "knot" of its unraveling physical laws.
3.  **Containment:** Using reality-calming technology reverse-engineered from [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]], the singularity is "lassoed" and stabilized within a **[[Causality Cage|Causality Cage]]**, preventing further decay or a dangerous vacuum collapse.
4.  **Processing:** The caged singularity is transported to a mobile **[[Forge-Station|Forge-Station]]** where it is subjected to a process of **[[Entropic Refinement|Entropic Refinement]]**, scrubbing it of unstable quantum fluctuations and residual temporal anomalies.
5.  **Coring:** The stabilized singularity is then encased in its final adamantine shell, creating the inert, transportable Singularity Core.

## Properties & Function

*   **Infinite Energy Potential:** A Core does not "produce" energy; it *is* energy in its purest, most condensed form. Siphon technology slowly converts its inherent mass into usable power via Hawking radiation-like processes, a controlled entropy that can power a civilization for millennia.
*   **Reality Anchor:** A stable Core exerts a calming influence on local physics, making it highly prized by [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] for patching **[[Reality Fractures|Reality Fractures]]**.
*   **Dimensional Stability:** Cores are used to power trans-reality travel and stabilize pocket dimensions, such as the [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]]'s headquarters, **[[The Ledger|The Ledger]]**.

## Applications

*   **Civilization-Scale Power:** A single Core can provide the entire energy needs of a Type III civilization indefinitely.
*   **Reality Engineering:** [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]] uses pure Cores to power **[[The Spire|The Spire]]** and mend damaged timelines.
*   **Propulsion:** Catalyst Cores are used in **[[Reality-Drives|Reality-Drives]]** for capital ships capable of moving between universes.
*   **Weaponization:** Volatile Cores can be engineered into **[[Vacuum Collapse Bombs|Vacuum Collapse Bombs]]**—weapons designed to trigger a localized, artificial heat death.

## Risks & Hazards

*   **Entropic Decay:** A damaged or improperly siphoning Core can leak catastrophic levels of energy, aging everything in its vicinity to dust in seconds.
*   **Reality Cascade:** A ruptured Core can unravel local spacetime, creating a permanent and expanding **[[Reality Tear|Reality Tear]]**.
*   **Theft & Misuse:** Catalyst Cores are highly sought after by warlords, mad scientists, and apocalypse cults. The [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]]'s **[[Compliance Division|Compliance Division]]** aggressively pursues lost or stolen Cores.

## The Market

Singularity Cores are the [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]]'s most valuable commodity. Their trade is heavily regulated by the company itself.
*   **Pricing:** A single Entropy-Pure Core is worth the entire annual GDP of a mid-tier galactic empire.
*   **Logistics:** Transporting Cores requires specialized **[[Null-Barge|Null-Barge]]** vessels and is insured by the [[Elysian Extractors & Acquisitions (EEA)|EEA]]'s in-house underwriters at exorbitant rates.
*   **Clients:** The primary buyers are stable, advanced civilizations, [[The Lux Chromatica|The Lux Chromatica]], and other multiversal megacorporations. Catalyst Cores are sold on a more discreet, "buyer-beware" basis.
