**The Sacred Texts** are a collection of 3 books. [[The Pulse Tract|The Pulse Tract]], [[The Émigré Manuscript|The Émigré Manuscript]], and [[The Necronomicon|The Necronomicon]]. 

All three books share many traits.
- They contain great power, both as collections of knowledge and as magical items.
- There exists only one original copy of each in the multi-verse.
- Any copies made will never be as strong as the source, both due to the original enchantments placed on each book and the intricate level of knowledge contained.
- The forbidden knowledge of contained within these books risks fracturing reader's minds
