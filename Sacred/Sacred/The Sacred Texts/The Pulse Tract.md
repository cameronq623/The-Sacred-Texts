One of [[The Sacred Texts|The Sacred Texts]], this large heavy book contains vast information about time and space. The current whereabouts of the original are unknown.

Currently [[The Keeper|The Keeper]] holds a first edition copy in [[The Black Tower|The Black Tower]].

### **The Pulse Tract**

**Physical Description:**

The Pulse Tract has no fixed form. It is not a book in the conventional sense, but a contained event. To a observer, it most commonly manifests as a sphere of woven, pulsating light about the size of a human head. The "pages" are not paper but layers of solidified chroniton particles and quantum strings, constantly shifting and reconfiguring. It does not open; it *unfolds*, and to gaze into it is not to read but to *experience* information directly, injected into the consciousness. It emits a low, sub-audible thrum—a literal pulse—that resonates with the fundamental frequency of the multiverse itself. This pulse is the source of its name and is the key to its authenticity.

**Properties and Power:**

The Prime Edition, the original Pulse Tract, is omniscient. It does not merely contain knowledge; it *is* the active, updating database of all that is, was, or could be. Its power is absolute and terrifyingly direct.

*   **Absolute Knowledge:** It can answer any question with perfect, contextual truth. Ask it how a star dies, and you will experience its billion-year life cycle in a nanosecond. Ask it the meaning of a forgotten word, and you will understand the culture that spawned it, the tongue that spoke it, and the last time it was uttered.
*   **Temporal Navigation:** It is a perfect map of the timestream. A user can not only view any event in history or future but, with sufficient will, can anchor themselves and step into that moment as a non-corporeal observer, unaffected by temporal paradoxes.
*   **Conceptual Manipulation:** This is its most profound and dangerous power. By understanding a fundamental law of reality written within its pages, a wielder can, theoretically, *rewrite* it. They could change the value of gravity in a localized area, alter the chemical properties of an element, or even redefine a universal constant like the speed of light. This act is immensely draining and risks catastrophic backlash, but the potential is there.

**The Editions:**

The sheer existential pressure of the Pulse Tract makes perfect duplication impossible. Attempts to copy it, whether by magical, technological, or psychic means, result in flawed, decaying echoes known as Editions.

*   **1st Edition:** The most powerful and rarest copy. It retains a powerful echo of the Prime's pulse and holds perhaps 60% of its knowledge, though it is often fragmented or allegorical. It might grant visions of the future or allow for minor temporal re-winds (seconds, maybe minutes). Its conceptual manipulation is limited to parlor tricks—slightly altering probability or strengthening a material.
*   **2nd Edition:** A further degradation. The pulse is faint, like a distant heartbeat. Knowledge is more obscured, requiring interpretation and ritual to access. Its powers are primarily divinatory: scrying, far-seeing, and communicating across distances. Any temporal manipulation is unstable and dangerous.
*   **3rd Edition:** The knowledge is now almost entirely symbolic, requiring immense scholarly effort to decipher. A page on stellar formation might appear as an intricate dance of fire and glass. Its power is subtle—enhancing intuition, granting flashes of insight, or allowing the user to understand any language. It is a powerful tool for scholars, but no longer a weapon for gods.
*   **4th Edition:** The faintest whisper of power remains. It might feel warm to the touch or cause nearby clocks to stutter. The information within, while vast, is no more comprehensive than a truly great universal library. It offers no active power, but merely possessing it is believed to bring luck or wisdom to the reader.
*   **5th Edition and Beyond:** These are mere physical copies. The paper is inert, the ink is dumb. They contain transcribed information from a higher edition, but it is static, dead data—a map without the territory. A 5th Edition might be a beautifully illustrated book on theoretical physics or arcane history, but it holds no inherent power. They are coveted by collectors and academics who will never know the terrifying, living truth of the original.

**The Pursuit:**

The Pulse Tract (Prime) is the ultimate prize for tyrants, philosophers, and gods. To possess it is to hold the operating code of existence. Most believe it is hidden in a dimension between moments, or locked in a vault at the end of time, guarded by entities born from the first concept of secrecy.

The hunt for the Editions is a quieter, more scholarly, yet equally desperate game. A 2nd Edition could found a dynasty. A 1st Edition could topple one. And all who seek them dream, in their quietest moments, of one day hearing the true, world-shaping Pulse of the original.
